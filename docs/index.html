02 / WHAT IS ACTUALLY RUNNING

One fixed clock.

Semi-implicit Euler at 1/120 s. The display accumulates real time, runs up to eight simulation steps per frame, and drops excess catch-up after slow frames. Hidden tabs pause.

A local solve.

For each joint, J = [-n, n]. The effective inverse mass is J M⁻¹ Jᵀ. Each iteration applies an impulse to reduce relative velocity and position drift. Circle contacts clamp normal impulses and apply Coulomb friction.

Know the limits.

Circles and center-to-center joints only. No angular dynamics, polygon collision, continuous collision detection or warm starting. Discrete fast bodies can tunnel. 160-body cap, O(n²) broad phase. Not a production physics engine.
